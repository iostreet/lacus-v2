What Lacus Does

From PDF to Knowledge Graph

Lacus reads your papers and builds a structured, queryable understanding of the research landscape — so you can see patterns across dozens of papers in minutes, not weeks.

Automated Extraction
Drop any research PDF and Lacus automatically parses title, authors, DOI, abstract, and year via GROBID — then runs deep AI extraction for materials, methods, properties, metrics, and findings.
Relation Graph
Extracted keywords become nodes in a rich graph — Materials link to Methods, Methods to Properties, Properties to Applications. See how concepts flow through each paper as a Story Map.
Multi-Paper Map View
Zoom out beyond individual papers. The Map View places all your papers on a shared canvas. Double-click to expand keyword sub-trees, draw connections between papers, and create custom concept nodes.
Performance Metrics
Numerical values — piezoelectric coefficients, Curie temperatures, efficiencies — are captured with units and measurement conditions, making quantitative comparison across papers instant.
Fully Editable
AI extraction is a starting point, not a black box. Edit keywords, relations, and metrics inline. Add notes in the memo field. Override any AI decision with a click and save back to the database.
Smart Filtering
The Map View filter bar auto-surfaces keywords shared by two or more papers. Toggle any keyword to instantly fade unrelated papers, or type your own filter terms to find thematic clusters.

Workflow

Ready in Four Steps

From raw PDF to structured knowledge in under a minute.

1
Import PDF
Click "Add Paper" or drag-and-drop a PDF from your local drive. The file stays on your machine.
2
AI Extraction
Lacus parses the paper and extracts keywords, relations, metrics, and a summary automatically.
3
Review & Edit
Open the detail panel to review and refine the extracted data — add notes, fix categories, adjust confidence.
4
Explore the Map
Switch to Map View to see all papers on a shared canvas, filter by keywords, and draw connections.

Copyright-Safe Design

Your PDFs Never Leave Your Machine

Lacus is architected from the ground up to respect copyright. The original paper files stay on your local computer — only the structured knowledge extracted from them is ever stored or transmitted.

Your Local Computer
PDF Files Stay Here
Original PDF Files The full text of every paper remains on your disk. Lacus reads it locally to perform extraction, then discards the raw content.
Full Paper Content Body text, figures, tables, and references are processed in memory during extraction — never written to any database or sent over a network.
Copyrighted Material Publisher-owned content never moves. You comply fully with journal and publisher terms of service because no protected content leaves your machine.
PDF ✕ Never
Local Database
Structured Knowledge Only
Paper Metadata Title, authors, year, journal, and DOI — all publicly available bibliographic information that carries no copyright restriction.
Extracted Analysis Results Keywords, relations, metrics, and summaries are AI-generated facts about the paper — your original research work, not the publisher's text.
Your Annotations & Notes Star ratings, memos, manual edits, map positions, and custom concept nodes — all created by you, owned by you.
Copyright Compliant by Design

Because Lacus stores only metadata and AI-extracted structured data — never the PDF itself or verbatim excerpts of protected text — it is designed to operate within the bounds of academic fair use and standard publisher terms of service. You retain full control: all data lives in a local SQLite file on your own machine.
