What Lacus Does

DOI-based Structure + Dialogue

Not a PDF repository. A living knowledge graph where every paper is a node in a growing network of ideas.

🗺
Research Flow Map
Visualize how your papers connect through shared materials, methods, and applications in a story-map layout.
🔬
Auto-extracted Structure
Keywords, metrics, themes, and concepts are extracted automatically — you review and refine.
💬
DOI-based Discussions
Every paper has a persistent discussion thread tied to its DOI, shared across the community.
🔒
Copyright-safe by Design
We store structure and metadata, never paper text. DOI-based references mean no copyright risk.

Privacy & Copyright

Your papers stay yours

Lacus never stores or distributes PDF content. All analysis happens on extracted metadata and user-assigned structure. DOI links point to the publisher — we only hold the knowledge graph you build.
