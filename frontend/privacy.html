Privacy & Copyright

Your papers stay yours

Lacus is designed to protect your research data and respect copyright from the ground up. Here is exactly what we store — and what we never touch.

Last updated: May 2025

📄 What We Store

Lacus only stores the structured metadata you choose to register. We do not accept, process, or retain PDF files or any full-text content of papers.

Paper title, year, journal, and authors — entered or confirmed by you.
DOI (Digital Object Identifier) — used to link to the publisher's page.
Auto-extracted keywords, themes, and concepts from the abstract (metadata only).
Your personal notes and relevance ratings — stored privately under your account.
DOI-based community comments — public and tied to the DOI, not the full paper.

🚫 What We Never Store

PDF files or any uploaded paper documents.
Full-text content, body paragraphs, or verbatim quotes from papers.
Figures, tables, or any visual content from publications.

🔒 Privacy by Account

Your personal research space is private by default. Only you can see your registered papers, notes, and knowledge graph.

Your memo and relevance score fields are visible only to you.
The community Knowledge Map shows anonymized aggregate keyword structure — not your individual papers or notes.
DOI-based comments are public and attached to your username.
You can delete your account and all associated data at any time.

⚖️ Copyright Policy

We respect the intellectual property of publishers and authors. Lacus is a knowledge graph tool, not a paper-sharing platform.

DOI links always point to the original publisher's page — we never host or proxy paper content.
We do not reproduce, excerpt, or index any copyrighted text from publications.
If you believe any content on Lacus infringes your copyright, please contact us immediately.

🍪 Cookies & Analytics

Lacus uses authentication cookies issued by Supabase to keep you signed in. We do not use third-party advertising trackers or sell your data to any third party.

Questions or Concerns?

If you have any questions about this policy, a copyright concern, or a request to delete your data, reach out to the operator directly.
