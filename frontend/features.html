What Lacus Does

DOI-based Structure + Dialogue

Not a PDF repository. A living knowledge graph where every paper is a node in a growing network of ideas.

🗺
Research Flow Map
Visualize how your papers connect through shared materials, methods, and applications in a story-map layout. Navigate upstream and downstream research paths at a glance.
🔬
Auto-extracted Structure
Keywords, metrics, themes, and concepts are extracted automatically from each paper you register. You review and refine — the structure is yours to own.
💬
DOI-based Discussions
Every paper has a persistent discussion thread tied to its DOI, shared across the community. The conversation stays with the paper — forever.
🔒
Copyright-safe by Design
We store structure and metadata, never paper text. DOI links point to the publisher — we only hold the knowledge graph you build.
🌐
Community Knowledge Map
The landing page shows a live map built from all registered papers — fields, themes, and concepts growing as the community contributes.
📌
Personal Research Space
Your papers, notes, and keyword graphs are private by default. You control what gets shared with the broader community map.

Privacy & Copyright

Your papers stay yours

Lacus never stores or distributes PDF content. All analysis happens on extracted metadata and user-assigned structure. DOI links point to the publisher — we only hold the knowledge graph you build.

No PDF storage — we never hold your full paper files.
Metadata only — title, authors, DOI, journal, year, and user-assigned keywords.
Your notes are private — the memo field is only visible to you.
DOI discussions are public — comments are tied to the DOI and visible to all.
Delete anytime — removing a paper removes all associated data from our servers.
